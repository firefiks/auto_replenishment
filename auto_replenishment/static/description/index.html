Auto Replenishment

Explainable replenishment recommendations based on sales quotations

Turn your open sales quotations into transparent, actionable replenishment signals. Instead of purchasing on gut feeling, Auto Replenishment computes a recommended order quantity for every saleable stock-tracked product and explains exactly why a product was flagged.

Nothing is bought automatically: the module only reports and optionally creates draft requests for quotation. You stay in control at every step.

How it works

A daily scheduled action walks every saleable stock-tracked product and aggregates quantities from sale.order.line:

  • Open quotations are weighted by a historical quotation-to-order conversion rate to estimate expected demand.
  • Window demand is computed over short / medium / long / longest periods (7 / 30 / 90 / 365 days) for confirmed orders and quotations alike.
  • A deterministic trend signal flags new or strongly increasing quotation activity.
  • The recommended quantity is:
    expected demand + safety stock − on hand − incoming, rounded up to the reordering rule's purchase multiple and the vendor's minimum quantity.
  • Every record stores a human readable reason: why the product was flagged, in plain language.

Actionable statuses

  • URGENT  recommended > 0, vendor set, on hand + incoming below safety stock
  • REPLENISH  recommended > 0 and a vendor is configured
  • DEMAND INCREASING  new or strongly growing quotation demand
  • WATCH  expected demand or missing vendor to replenish
  • NORMAL  no action needed

Reported where you work

Actionable products are posted to a Discuss channel (#AutoReplenishment, created automatically). Lines are color-coded by recommended quantity — 5 or more red, 1–4 orange, below 1 black — and sorted by urgency. Anti-spam keeps the channel clean: a product is re-posted only when its signal changed or the report interval elapsed.

A boolean toggle restricts reporting to actionable statuses only (URGENT + REPLENISH) so your buyers see exactly what needs an order.

Create draft RFQs in one click

The Create Draft RFQ button generates a purchase.order in draft for the preferred vendor, with the planned date based on vendor delay and quantities respecting minimums and purchase multiples. Duplicate requests are refused when an open order already covers the product. Nothing is confirmed or sent to vendors.

Configuration

All behaviour is tuned from Inventory → Configuration → Settings → Auto Replenishment:

  • Custom demand windows and trend window length
  • Fallback conversion rate, minimum conversion sample and DEMAND INCREASING growth threshold
  • Safety stock level
  • Channel filter (All / Webshop / Manual / Other)
  • Optional auto-create draft RFQs by the cron (off by default — still never confirmed)
  • Discuss integration toggle, channel name and report interval

Features at a glance

  • Transparent math: every recommendation is computed from live sales data with a stored, human readable reason.
  • Quotation-driven: captures demand from open quotations, not only confirmed orders.
  • Color-coded Discuss reporting with anti-spam and configurable scope.
  • Draft RFQ only: no automatic ordering, no vendor confirmation — you decide.
  • Deterministic: no black-box forecasting, easy to audit and explain to colleagues.
  • Multi-company aware and isolated per warehouse setup.
  • Fully tested: 25 automated tests ship with the module (LGPL-3).
